This website keeps its use of cookies and browser storage to a minimum.

## What we use

- **Session storage for booking:** your browser notes whether you have already opened or completed a booking in this visit. It holds no personal details and is cleared when you close the tab.
- **No advertising or tracking cookies.** We do not run analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

## Third parties

- **Google Maps:** the map on the contact page only loads when you tap "Show map". After you open it, Google can place its own cookies, covered by Google's privacy terms.
- **Booking system:** when you confirm a booking, your browser talks directly to ClinicFlow247, the clinic's booking system, to send your code and save the appointment.

## Your control

Your browser settings let you wipe or refuse cookies and stored site data. The site still works; you may simply need to start a booking again.
