*Draft for the clinic's review.*

Everything on this website, including the treatment pages, department pages and patient guides, is general information written to help you understand common problems and what to expect at a visit.

- Reading it is **not the same as being examined**; only a qualified doctor or dentist who sees you can diagnose a problem.
- Every person is different. How a treatment is planned, how long it takes and how you respond to it depends on your own health; **results vary**, and no outcome is promised.
- Do not stop, start or change any medicine because of something you read here. Ask your doctor.
- Children's health and eye-care pages are general guidance for families, not instructions for a particular child or condition.

**Emergencies** such as breathing trouble, chest pain, a bad injury, a fit, a child you cannot rouse or vision that vanishes suddenly belong in a hospital casualty department straight away; dial 108 for an ambulance. Do not wait for a clinic appointment.

Patient guides are being reviewed by the clinic's doctors; until then they are marked as awaiting review.
