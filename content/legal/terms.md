## Appointments booked online

- An online booking reserves a time with the doctor you chose. The clinic may occasionally need to move it; we will contact you if so.
- Please give accurate details, including a mobile number you can be reached on. One booking is for one patient.
- If you cannot come, call the clinic so the time can go to someone else.
- Patients in pain or with urgent problems may be seen ahead of booked times.
- A request sent by WhatsApp or as an enquiry is not confirmed until the clinic replies.
