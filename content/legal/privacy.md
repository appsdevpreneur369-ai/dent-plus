Dent Plus Multi Speciality Dental & Family Clinic ("Dent Plus", "we") runs this website and treats patients at D.No 5-72-31/3, Ashok Nagar 3rd Lane, Lakshmipuram, Guntur 522007. This page explains what personal data we handle through the website and why.

## What we collect

- **When you book or send a request:** your name, mobile number, email address, the department, doctor and concern you choose, and the date and time you pick.
- **When you verify your number:** your mobile number is sent a one-time code by SMS through our booking provider.
- **When you contact us** by phone or WhatsApp: whatever you choose to tell us.
- **Technical data:** short-lived server records, for example your IP address and browser, used to keep the website secure and running.

We do not ask for medical records through this website. Please do not send them by WhatsApp unless the clinic asks you to.

## Why we use it

- to set up your appointment, confirm it, move it if needed and send reminders
- to contact you about your visit and send follow-up messages after it
- to keep the clinic's appointment and patient records, as health-care law requires
- to protect the website against misuse

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Who processes it for us

Bookings, reminders, prescriptions and follow-up messages are handled by **ClinicFlow247**, the clinic's booking and practice-management system, which stores the data on our behalf. SMS and WhatsApp messages are delivered through its messaging partners. The map on our contact page comes from Google and stays switched off until you tap to show it.

## How long we keep it

Clinical and appointment records stay on file for the period that Indian law and medical-records guidance set. Enquiries that do not lead to a visit are deleted when no longer needed.

## Your choices and rights

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to view, fix or delete your data, to stop messages by withdrawing consent, and to complain if something goes wrong. Speak to reception or call the clinic. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date at the top.
